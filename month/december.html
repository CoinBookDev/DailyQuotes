WHAT REMAINS

Some things never really leave us.

Introduction

A year is made of moments we don't realise we're living. Ordinary afternoons, conversations that last longer than expected, people we meet, places we visit, and experiences that quietly become part of who we are. Time moves forward whether we're ready or not. People change, memories fade, and things that once felt permanent eventually become part of the past. But perhaps not everything is meant to stay. Some moments are valuable precisely because they don't last forever. December is a time to look back. Not necessarily to measure what we've achieved, but to remember what we've experienced, what we've learned, and what has stayed with us. Because sometimes, what remains is more important than what we've lost.
